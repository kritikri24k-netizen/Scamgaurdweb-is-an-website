SafeGuard Comprehensive System Guide

Complete walkthrough of every feature in SafeGuard, followed by an interactive multi-scenario simulator trial.

1. Recruiter Messaging & Chat Inspection

Paste transcripts from WhatsApp, Telegram, LinkedIn, or SMS. The engine checks across 5 regional languages (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Bengali, Malayalam) for artificial urgency, task bait, and progressive pressure tactics.

2. Corporate Domain vs Free Webmail Audit

Audits recruiter sender emails against official company websites. Automatically flags legitimate brand names using free public webmails like @gmail.com or @yahoo.com with missing joining dates or ambiguous stipends.

3. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Upload

Upload scanned PDFs or image-based appointment letters (PNG, JPG). Extracts text directly from documents to reveal disguised security deposits, laptop caution fees, and equipment charges.

4. QR Code & UPI Collection Traps

Upload screenshots of payment QR codes or UPI prompts. Alerts users that scanning a QR code or entering a UPI PIN strictly deducts funds and never receives bonuses or payments.

5. Voice Recording Transcription & Analysis

Upload phone recordings or voice notes. Transcribes spoken dialogue and detects urgency markers, phone interview fee requests, and identity disclosure pressure.

6. Scammer Database & Fuzzy Matching

Community-reported registry with 3-tier risk index (<5 Low, 5–9 Medium, ≥10 High Risk). Normalized Levenshtein similarity flags offenders even if they slightly alter their name or handle representation.

7. History Storage & Inline Accuracy Ratings

Signed-in accounts preserve scan logs, risk summaries, and timestamps. Submit 1-to-5 star ratings directly beside scan results to help train and verify model precision.

8. National Helpline (1930) Direct Routing

Direct routing to the 24x7 Citizen Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline (1930) and the national reporting portal (cybercrime.gov.in) to dispute unauthorized transactions in the golden hour.
