Fraudsters pose as tech startups or MNC HRs offering paid internships. Before issuing the appointment letter, they ask for a "refundable gate pass fee", "laptop insurance", or "document processing charge" of ₹500 to ₹3,000 via UPI. Genuine employers never charge candidates.
You are asked to like YouTube videos, write Google Maps reviews, or book hotels with promised daily returns of ₹1,500 to ₹5,000. Early payouts of ₹150 are made to build trust, followed by demands for ₹10,000 "crypto or merchant recharge" before releasing earnings.
Recruiters message you on LinkedIn or Naukri, then immediately pressure you to move the conversation to personal Telegram or WhatsApp handles to bypass platform anti-abuse screening.
Letters sent from addresses like `tcs-recruitment-team@gmail.com` or `infosys-careers@yahoo.com`. Official corporate recruiters communicate strictly through registered company domain names.
Demanding clear front and back photographs of Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, canceled cheques, and bank passbooks before any formal screening or job offer has been issued.
Claiming they are "transferring your signing bonus or stipend" by sending a QR code or collect request. Remember: scanning a QR code or entering your UPI PIN always deducts money; it never receives money.
